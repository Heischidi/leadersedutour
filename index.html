Everything you need to know about international admissions, visa procedures, scholarships, and our consultancy services.

Our initial profile assessment and counselling session is 100% free with no obligation. During this 30-minute consultation, our certified advisors evaluate your academic background, discuss career goals, recommend top-fit countries and institutions, and outline a clear step-by-step roadmap.

We have a proven track record of overturning previous visa refusals. Our senior advisors conduct a thorough forensic review of your refusal letter (GCMS notes for Canada, refusal notice for UK/USA/Australia), pinpoint the underlying deficiencies, and prepare a restructured application with stronger supporting documentation and an explanatory statement.

In Canada, students can work off-campus up to 24 hours per week during academic sessions. In Australia, students can work up to 48 hours per fortnight. All three destinations also provide generous post-graduation work permits.

Not always! Many partner universities in the UK, Europe, and Canada accept a WAEC/NECO English grade of C6 or better in place of IELTS. Others offer university-specific online English placement tests or accept evidence that your previous degree was taught entirely in English (Medium of Instruction letter). We help identify universities that offer English waivers.

Scholarship amounts vary by country, university, and individual profile. Most of our partner institutions offer automatic early-bird or merit bursaries ranging from £2,000 to £10,000 (or $3,000 to $15,000). Highly qualified students with strong academic records or research proposals can access competitive full-tuition or government-funded scholarships.

We recommend starting 6 to 9 months before your preferred intake (for example, starting in January or February for a September intake). This provides ample time for university offer processing, scholarship applications, financial proof consolidation, CAS/I-20 issuance, and student visa processing without unnecessary stress.
